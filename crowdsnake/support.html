Crowd Snake — Support

Crowd Snake: Paint Planets is a casual territory game: your crowd follows you like a snake, gates change its size and closed loops paint the planet in your color.

FAQ

How do I capture land? Leave your territory, draw a loop with your crowd and come back home. Everything inside the loop becomes yours. Don't let rivals cut your chain while it's outside!

What do the gates do? Green gates add people (+N), golden gates multiply them (×N). A bigger crowd wins clashes.

What is the season pass? During a season (for example, the "Liftoff" launch season) wins, retries and daily gifts fill 15 tiers with coins and exclusive hats. The optional Premium track adds more rewards.

I bought something but don't see it. Open the map, tap the coin counter to enter the Shop and tap "Restore purchases". "No Ads" and the Premium pass are restored automatically on the same Apple Account.

What does "No Ads" remove? Full-screen ads between levels. Rewarded videos (for example ×3 coins) stay optional.

Is my progress synced? Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the game deletes it.
