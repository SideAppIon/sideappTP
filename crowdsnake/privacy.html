Crowd Snake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Crowd Snake: Paint Planets ("the game") does not require an account and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts or precise location.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, coins, hats, season pass progress and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Analytics

To understand how the game is played and improve it, we use Unity Analytics (Unity Gaming Services, Unity Technologies). The game sends usage events — sessions, levels started, won or lost, rewards claimed, season pass progress, ads shown and which in-app product was bought — together with a random player ID generated by the game, app version, device model, iOS version, language and country setting. Unity may derive your approximate location from the connection's IP address. We do not send the advertising identifier (IDFA), your name or any contact data with these events.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded videos and occasional full-screen ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To serve and measure ads, Unity may collect device identifiers (the IDFA only if you allow tracking in the system prompt), IP address, device model, OS version and ad-interaction data. You can refuse tracking in the prompt or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Buying "No Ads" turns off full-screen ads. See Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details; the game only learns which product was bought so it can deliver it.

Season schedule

To know whether a season (and its pass) is running, the game downloads a small schedule file from this website (hosted on GitHub Pages). Like any web request it reveals your IP address to the host; the file request carries no personal data and we do not use it to identify you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
