Flipside Racer — Support

Flipside Racer is a one-thumb arcade racer with 25 tracks in 6 worlds, 10 cars and nine rivals in every race.

FAQ

How do I control the car? The car accelerates by itself. Drag left or right to steer, hold the brake button for tight bends and tap the nitro button when the road is clear.

How do I unlock new tracks? Tracks open one by one — finish a track on the podium (top 3) to open the next one.

How do I get more nitro? Drifts, near misses, smashing obstacles and shoving rivals refill nitro. You can also fill the tank completely by watching an optional short video.

How do I buy a new car? Open the Garage and spend the coins you earn in races, daily rewards and missions. No real money is needed.

I crashed — is it game over? No. There are no lives: a crash only slows you down for a moment.

How do I change music, sounds, vibration or language? Home screen → settings button.

How do I turn off anonymous statistics? Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous stats". "Delete my analytics data" there also resets your anonymous identifier.

Is my progress synced? Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.
