Tap It More — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Tap It More: Idle Tycoon ("the game") does not require an account and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts or precise location.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, company name, settings and streak are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Analytics

To understand how the game is played and improve it, we use Unity Analytics (Unity Gaming Services, Unity Technologies). The game sends anonymous usage events — when a session starts and ends, a periodic "game is running" signal and which ads were shown — together with a random player ID generated by the game, app version, device model, iOS version and language. Unity may derive your approximate country from the connection's IP address. We do not send the advertising identifier (IDFA), your name or any contact data with these events. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent on first launch and sends nothing until you accept. Everywhere you can turn analytics off at any time in the game: Settings → Analytics. To request deletion of analytics data already collected, email us your Player ID shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded videos and occasional full-screen ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). To serve and measure ads, Unity may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device model, OS version and ad-interaction data. Ads are personalized only with your consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks on first launch, and everywhere you can switch personalized ads off in the game (Settings → Personalized ads); you will then see non-personalized ads and the game will not ask for tracking permission. You can also refuse tracking in the system prompt or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (for example, when your offline earnings are full or your daily bonus is ready) are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
