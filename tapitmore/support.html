Tap It More: Idle Tycoon is an idle clicker where you grow a business empire from a coffee cart to a Mars colony.

FAQ

How do I earn more? Tap the big button to run your current business, upgrade businesses with the Buy button and hire a manager so a business earns on its own — even while you're away.

What are gems for? Gems come from boss quests, daily bonuses and the comeback chest. Spend them in the Shop on boosts, time warps and a bigger offline vault.

What does going public (IPO) do? It restarts your run with investors who permanently boost all profits. Gems, investors, Golden Contracts and your rank are kept.

How long do I earn offline? Up to the offline vault limit shown in the game. You can raise it in the Shop.

How do I turn off music, sound or vibration? Settings (gear icon) → Music, Sound, Haptics.

Is my progress synced? Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the game deletes it.
