Logic Mate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Logic Mate ("the app") is a brain-training puzzle game. We do not require an account and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address or contacts.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, XP, streak, settings and reminder time) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Analytics

To understand which puzzles are too hard or too easy, the app sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies): app sessions, levels started and completed, stars, mistakes, hints used, ads watched, device model, iOS version, country and language. These events are tied to a random identifier generated on your device — never your name, email or advertising identifier — and are not used to track you across other companies' apps.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → "Share anonymous statistics". Turning it off stops all analytics and sends Unity a request to delete the data already collected for your identifier.

Advertising

The app may show ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), for example an optional video in exchange for a hint. Unity may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. You can refuse tracking in the system prompt or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
