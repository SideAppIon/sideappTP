Logic Mate — Support

Logic Mate is a minimalist brain-training app: 100 levels of classic puzzles, a daily challenge, ranks and achievements.

FAQ

How do I get a hint? Tap the light bulb at the top right of a level. Hints are offered for an optional short video.

I missed a day — is my streak lost? Streak freezes (earned on every rank-up, up to 3) are used automatically for missed days.

How do I change reminders, sounds or vibration? Home screen → settings button (top right).

Is my progress synced? Progress is stored on your device only.
