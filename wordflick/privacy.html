WordFlick — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

WordFlick ("the app") is a vocabulary flashcard app. We do not require an account and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address or contacts. We do not run any servers that receive your data.

Data stored on your device

Your words, decks, learning progress, streak, settings and the name you optionally enter during onboarding are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, study reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Pronunciation

Words are spoken with Apple's built-in speech synthesis on your device. No audio or text is sent to us.

Purchases

WordFlick Pro subscriptions, the lifetime purchase and streak freezes are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether a purchase is active.

Advertising

The free version shows ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): occasional full-screen ads between study sessions — never during a session — and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward (double XP, a streak freeze or a Pro deck for 24 hours). Unity may collect device identifiers (such as the IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. You can refuse tracking in the system prompt or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. WordFlick Pro shows no ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
