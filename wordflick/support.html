WordFlick — Support

WordFlick is a vocabulary flashcard app for 15 languages with 1,000 built-in words, animated picture associations and 5-minute sessions.

FAQ

How do I study? Tap Start on the Home screen. Swipe right if you know the word, left if you don't and down if it was hard. Words you miss come back sooner.

How are new words added? Pick topics in the Library. Each day a few new words from them join your sessions; you can change the number of new words per day in Settings. In a deck you can switch off words you already know or don't need.

How do I add my own words? Library → "+" to create a deck, then "+" inside the deck to add a word with a translation, an emoji or symbol and an animation.

How do I change the language I'm learning? Settings → language. Progress on built-in words starts over for the new language.

What does WordFlick Pro include? All decks, unlimited mode, unlimited custom decks, up to 5 streak freezes and no ads.

How do I restore purchases? Open the Pro screen and tap "Restore".

How do I cancel a subscription? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → WordFlick.

Is my progress synced? Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.
